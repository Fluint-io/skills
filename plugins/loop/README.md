# Loop by Fluint

Eleven sales plays that run enterprise deals the way top reps sell. Each play is one moment in a deal, built on the frameworks from *Selling With* and *Brief & Brilliant*, reads the deal from Loop, and ends in something you can send, written in your buyer's own words.

| Play | What you walk away with |
|------|-------------------------|
| `/loop:plays` | Not sure what you need? Describe the deal and it points you to the right play. |
| `/loop:call-prep` | A one-screen meeting brief: a point of view, planned discovery, the opening, and the ask. |
| `/loop:follow-up` | A stakeholder map, the problem in the buyer's words, and a forwardable follow-up for each person on the call. |
| `/loop:multithread` | The real buying committee, a value hypothesis for each person, and the outreach to reach them. |
| `/loop:account-plan` | A point of view, the opportunities ranked by executive priority, and the first message to send. |
| `/loop:champion` | The kit your champion carries into the meetings you are not in. |
| `/loop:displace` | A plan to unseat the incumbent by changing the criteria, not by winning a feature war. |
| `/loop:business-case` | A one-page business case in the customer's words, scored, with the next move to strengthen it. |
| `/loop:advance` | Why the deal stalled and the moves to restart it, or a mutual action plan to close it. |
| `/loop:qbr` | A QBR that leads with their success, gets ahead of risk, and opens expansion without a pitch. |
| `/loop:manage-up` | A forecast-call pack: an honest read of the territory, your big bets, and specific asks of leadership. |

You do not have to remember any of these. Say what you are doing, such as *"Prep me for my call with Acme tomorrow"*, and the right play runs. Pair it with **Olli by Fluint** to talk a deal through first.

## Requirements

- A Fluint account.
- The Loop connector, `https://mcp.fluint.io/mcp`, connected and signed in with your Fluint account. The plays have no other data source and stop if Loop is not connected.

## What it reads and sends

The plays are instructions and reference material only. They run no code, scripts, hooks, or local servers on your machine.

- **Reads:** deal, meeting, email, and activity briefs from your Fluint workspace, through the Loop connector, using your own Fluint sign-in. Those briefs come from your connected CRM, call recordings, and email, and can include names, titles, and email addresses of the people on your deals.
- **Sends:** your questions go to Loop to find the relevant briefs. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and the plugin stores nothing of its own. Drafts the plays write stay in your conversation until you choose to send them.
