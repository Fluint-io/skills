# Uncommon Sales Skills

Ten Claude skills that run enterprise sales plays the way top reps actually sell: in the buyer's own language, sourced from your live deal data, and ending in a forwardable asset.

Built by [Nate Nasralla](https://www.fluint.io), author of *Selling With* and *Brief & Brilliant*.

## What makes them different

1. **Buyer's language, not vendor voice.** Camouflage, not a billboard.
2. **A real methodology**, not a bag of prompt tricks.
3. **An operator's read** on every output: the judgment a summary hides.
4. **A deliverable every time.** You finish with the asset, ready to send.
5. **Your real data**, sourced Loop-first with graceful fallback.

See [[The Philosophy]] for the why behind each of these.
